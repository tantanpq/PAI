# Founder

## Tân — creator of PAI

I am building **PAI — Personal Assistant Intelligence**: user-owned intelligence designed to preserve continuity, understand goals, continue useful work across time, and verify outcomes against evidence.

My interest is not in building another chatbot interface or an agent swarm for its own sake. I am interested in what happens when AI can become a durable extension of a person's capability without taking ownership away from that person.

## What I am trying to solve

Most AI experiences are still temporary. Context is fragmented across chats, tools and providers. Users repeatedly reconstruct decisions, restate goals, move information between systems and verify whether generated work actually produced the intended result.

PAI is my attempt to reduce that burden.

The project explores how an assistant can:

- maintain continuity across time;
- preserve user intent and important decisions;
- turn long-running objectives into resumable work;
- use multiple models and tools without making one provider the permanent owner of the user's intelligence;
- verify results with evidence;
- learn from qualified outcomes;
- become more proactive while staying inside explicit user authority.

## What I believe

Technology should ultimately make life better for people.

AI should give users more time, capability, ownership and freedom to act. It should reduce repetitive coordination and cognitive overhead instead of creating a new layer of systems that constantly demand supervision.

Powerful models matter, but they are not the whole system. Continuity, evidence, privacy, reusable capability and clear authority boundaries matter just as much if AI is going to remain useful over years rather than minutes.

## What is public today

The public PAI repository currently includes three executable capability surfaces:

- **Context Kit 0.1.0** for deterministic bounded context compilation;
- **W_Flow Core 0.1.0** for replayable provider-neutral work-loop semantics;
- **SimLab Core 0.1.0 public pilot** for deterministic synthetic verification.

It also contains public Skills, runbooks, workflows, patterns, case studies and reliability evidence.

The complete **PAI Personal** product is still under development and is not presented as a supported public release today.

## Longer-term direction

The central direction is PAI Personal: an assistant that can remain continuous with one user, understand the current trajectory, resume meaningful work and increasingly handle bounded execution without requiring the user to manually coordinate every step.

Other future directions may include team/work continuity, professional-domain intelligence, business capabilities, private/local execution and eventually physical-world capability when maturity and safety justify it.
